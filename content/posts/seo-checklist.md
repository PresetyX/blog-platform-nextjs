Search engines reward pages that are fast, clear and well described.

## Checklist

- A unique `<title>` and meta description per page
- Canonical URLs and Open Graph tags
- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`
- An RSS feed
- JSON-LD `BlogPosting` structured data

This blog generates all of them from the markdown front matter.
